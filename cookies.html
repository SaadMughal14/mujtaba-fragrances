Cookie Policy

Mujtaba Fragrances • Digital Gallery Experience

1. Understanding Our Cookies

Mujtaba Fragrances utilizes small text tokens ("cookies") and local storage technologies to elevate your digital experience. These essential technologies preserve your shopping cart state, recall your currency choices, and ensure flawless responsive rendering of our luxury gallery artwork.

2. Categories of Cookies We Employ

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Required to enable core navigation, session continuity, secure checkout transactions, and bag persistence.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Measure visitor interactions and page loading speeds anonymously to refine website responsiveness.
  • Preference & Experience Cookies: Store personalized preferences such as currency, theme selection, and previously viewed flacons.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You may adjust your browser settings at any time to block, limit, or remove cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to add fragrances to your shopping bag or complete checkout seamlessly.
